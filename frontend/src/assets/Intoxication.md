# **Intoxication**
<br>

This is something I've been trying to write for a while. This happens to most of us, knowingly or unknowingly we indulge ourselvs in the act of self-sabotage caused mostly by our exhaused brain and mind. But we never call it self-sabotage, rather we coined a fancy term for it...Escape, and with every passing day, we are trapping ourselves worse than before with the failed attempts of escape from ourselves.

To escape, we drink. We smoke. We laugh...just enough to let a fraction of that emptiness out so that it can be filled with a heavier burden. We take breaks, do nothing, scroll through that illuminated screen long enough to lose a part of our soul. We validate these rituals, fill ourselves with broken promises and we repeat. 

Nothing changes. 

Perhaps self-sabotage is so engaging that it tricks our brain into believing that we are doing just fine. A single productive day fails to hide the decay underneath. An exhausted brain and a hopeful heart cannot coexist. 

In a world, where everything else seems to have found their purpose of living, why are we behind? Maybe we are still wandering or Maybe this very act of self-sabotage is nothing but a medium. An opportunity to explore ourselves. 

But what if there is nothing else to look for...except a mirror! 

A mirror that reflects the naked us. The angry us. The happy us. The broken us...the real us and it keeps on showing. 

The moment we come across these versions, we question them. We question their very existance! But how dare we...when we are the ones who brought them back to life? 

This intoxicated self, our very own creation, now ready to walk the path with us toward the void. 

The numerous pointless justifications we feed ourselves don't answer back. We intoxicate ourselves to justfy who we have become.
And the worst part? We take pride in it.

For me, this game of intoxication started with a woman. My reason for intoxication. And I wouldn't have believed it myself that sometimes intoxication can bring out something better out of you. 

Living off caffine, plunged into screamy rock music, tattoos declaring solidarity and Dostoevsky extracting something out of me. 

I can't name what emerged. 
I can't ignore it. 
I can't face it yet I can't shake it away from me. 

It answers my late night thoughts. 
Walks with me everytime I'm walking alone. 
Talks to me every time I talk to myself. 

Everytime I think of letting go it is the one who challange me for a duel and I can't help but surrender. 

It lives off me. 

Whenever, I ask a specific question to myself, it whispers into my soul and I know, this guy wouldn't have existed otherwise. I can't help but smirk.

The more I enjoy its company, the more it tries to take over. It loves cheap dopamine. One wrong move and it won't think twice before destorying me. 

This is the price I pay. 

I've intoxicated myself long enough to create something I now fear. 

It is the only stranger who shows you the right path, then take away everything else, leaving itself and me, forever. 

Yet, there is only one way to get rid of it. 

To create something far more dangerous.

A monster that won't agree to my madness and the moment I stop working on myself, it'll devour me. 

Sometimes, if I just sit still for a moment I could hear both of them arguing over my possession from the deepest corner of my soul...while keeping an eye on me. 

One showed me what I am. 
The other promises what I could become. 

Abandoing either, I'll end up betraying a part of myself.

Trying to trace a ray of hope from the inside of a cacoon entangled with my thoughts and action and then...I saw a narrow shadowed reflection in the mirror. 

Surprisingly, I couldn't recognize this person right away.

It's someone from 2 years back. A guy with short hair, trimmed beared, comparatively thinner, joyous for some unknown reason and a head full of nonsense. 

Good old days. 

I smirked and looked away, and then when I looked again at the mirror I was shocked, I know this guy as well but he is just one year younger than me. Long hair, beard covering the lower part of the face, baggy eyes, probabbly holding a Dostoevsky or Kafka or Nietzche, unhappy for obvious reasons and the head? It's worse than before. 

The moment the mirror was about to show another reflection, a crack appeared on the surface. 

Maybe the mirror couldn't handle what appeared before it. Maybe, even the mirror is tired to showing the skins I've been wearing for all these years. Maybe the mirror makes me remember everyday that had I been given what I once thrived for, I would never have become the monster I am today. 

But, somewhere...I need to believe that it's for the greater good.

As my mind agonizes me the with the scenarios where I had it all, it screams at me for not being enough, it screams at me...for letting go. Yet it praises me only when I'm severly injured and keep pushing forward. It loves to see the sufferings. I know! I know this too well! It never fails to devour the pain. 

When you're wired this way, anything painless feels unworthy.

My demons taught me that as well. 

And maybe that's where it all began. 

The tug-o-war between the demon I am and the demon I could be. Where I can expect love as a feeling and not as a materialistic aspect, where my awareness and my feelings can coexist. But everytime I wish for this, I realize that I don't truly want it. 

I need stillness as an eternal companion of my mind. 

A sharper intelligence. 

A new demon.

The castle I've built over the years, stands still. It just needs a new host. Why I couldn't overpower my demon yet? I knew the answer all along. 

I can't defeat this version as it is the truest form of myself.

The answers to my prayers kept knocking on my door and I mistook them for intoxication. Some layers settled atop of my true self...sharp enough to make others very uncomfortable. 

While writing this on the night of 31st December, sitting in front of the white screen and when every keystroke from my keyboard was hitting on my concisousness, I could hear people welcoming the new year with open arms but somewhere in between I was looking for a reason to join them, yet I couldn't find one. 

My demon has claimed the most of me. 

That craving is gone. 

In the search of what I truly am, I played this long game of trial and error and the tables have turned in ways I never imagined.

When we cross a line to acquire something, to achieve something, it's believed that the devil has answered to our prayers. But the lucrative side of the outcome attracts us without giving us a warning about the concequences and everytime we make a successful trade...we sell a part of ourselves in form of health, patience, integrity...sometimes worse. We call it sacrifice. But sometimes, it's intoxication. And the conversation circles back to one of the core aspects. 

It's an eternal cycle of conflict.

The damage is irreversible. But the relief remains in the fact that I've discovered myself.
